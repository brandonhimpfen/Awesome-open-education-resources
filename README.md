> A curated list of resources for Open Educational Resources (OER), open textbooks, free courseware, open licensing tools, and OER search engines.

Open Educational Resources (OER) are freely accessible, openly licensed text, media, and digital assets that are useful for teaching, learning, and assessing as well as for research purposes. This list is intended for librarians, educators, instructional designers, academic leads, and independent self-learners.

## Open Textbooks & Repositories

Platforms hosting peer-reviewed, open-access digital textbooks and modular learning materials across academic disciplines.

- [OpenStax](https://openstax.org/) — Non-profit educational initiative publishing peer-reviewed, openly licensed college and High School textbooks.
- [Open Textbook Library](https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks) — Comprehensive catalog of open textbooks reviewed by faculty across higher education institutions.
- [LibreTexts](https://libretexts.org/) — Multi-institutional collaborative platform developing open-access educational resources and interactive textbooks.
- [BCcampus OpenEd](https://open.bccampus.ca/) — Curated collection of open textbooks aligned with post-secondary course offerings and vocational trades.

## Open Courseware (OCW) Platforms

Freely available university course materials, syllabi, lecture notes, and assignments open to global learners.

- [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/) — Web-based publication of virtually all MIT course content, accessible to the public without registration.
- [Carnegie Mellon Open Learning Initiative (OLI)](https://oli.cmu.edu/) — Openly accessible online courses offering targeted feedback and data-driven learning modules.
- [OER Commons](https://www.oercommons.org/) — Public digital library of open educational resources supporting collaborative curriculum building.

## OER Search Engines & Aggregators

Specialized discovery tools for searching and indexing open educational assets across multi-university digital repositories.

- [MOM (Mason OER Metafinder)](https://oer.gmu.edu/mam-mason-oer-metafinder/) — Real-time federated search engine querying over 20 major OER repositories simultaneously.
- [MERLOT](https://www.merlot.org/) — Curated collection of peer-reviewed online learning materials, administrative tools, and content authoring guides.
- [OASIS (Openly Available Sources Integrated Search)](https://oasis.geneseo.edu/) — User-friendly search tool indexing open textbooks, courses, audiobooks, and interactive modules.

## Licensing & Authoring Tools

Frameworks, tools, and platforms designed to publish, remix, and correctly license open educational assets.

### Creative Commons & Open Licenses

- [Creative Commons Chooser](https://creativecommons.org/choose/) — Interactive tool for selecting and embedding appropriate open copyright licenses onto work.
- [Open Definition Framework](https://opendefinition.org/) — Principles defining openness in data and content to guarantee seamless remixing capabilities.

### OER Authoring Platforms

- [Pressbooks](https://github.com/pressbooks/pressbooks) — Open-source book creation software optimized for publishing accessible, multi-format open textbooks.
